TOUCH SAMPLE KIT - READ ME FIRST

This sets up a pretend MPC Sample on your Mac. It needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer),
an internet connection, and about 15 GB of free space.

1. Put "MPC Sample 1.3.0 Updater" (the .zip or the .app you downloaded from Akai) on your Desktop.
   No Akai files are inside this kit; the emulator is built from your own copy.
2. Unzip this kit (double-click the zip).
3. Right-click "Setup" (Setup.command) and choose Open, then Open again. A Terminal window appears.
   If macOS refuses, go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and press "Open Anyway".
4. Follow the window. It may ask for your Mac password (typing shows nothing - that is normal).
   The first run takes 30-60 minutes. Leave the window open until it says "All done".
   If it stops with a message, fix that, then run Setup again: it carries on where it stopped.
5. Afterwards, double-click "Start MPC Sample" on your Desktop. Your browser opens the MPC screen.
   To quit, click the Terminal window and press Control-C (it saves and shuts down).

Your samples go in:  Documents/Codex/TouchSample/vendor/virtual-mpc-sample/sdcard/

Note: there is no MPC Touch screen needed; the on-screen page in the browser is the panel.
